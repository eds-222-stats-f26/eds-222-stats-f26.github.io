Fit and interpret a regression model

Fit the model

Use lm() to fit a regression model to your simulated data.

Tip

lm() expects a formula stating your predictor and response variables. It should look like: response ~ predictor.

your_model <- lm(your_model_formula, data = your_simulated_data)
